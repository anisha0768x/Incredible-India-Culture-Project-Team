Visual Arts

Explore the diverse and vibrant world of Indian visual arts, spanning ancient cave paintings to contemporary installations, including painting, sculpture, textile arts, and more.

Traditional Painting Styles

Madhubani Painting

Ancient tradition
Bihar

Characterized by eye-catching geometrical patterns and natural dyes. Originally created by women on mud walls.

Warli Art

2500 BCE onwards
Maharashtra

Tribal art using basic geometric shapes like circles, triangles and squares to depict daily life.

Tanjore Painting

16th century CE
Tamil Nadu

Known for rich colors, gold foil work, and glass bead embellishments depicting Hindu deities.

Sculpture & Architecture

Khajuraho Sculptures

10th-12th century CE
Madhya Pradesh

Famous for intricate erotic sculptures that represent various aspects of life in medieval India.

Ellora Cave Sculptures

6th-10th century CE
Maharashtra

34 cave monuments carved out of Charanandri hills, showcasing Hindu, Buddhist and Jain art.

Konark Sun Temple

13th century CE
Odisha

Shaped like a giant chariot with intricately carved wheels, pillars and walls depicting various scenes.

Textile & Craft Arts

Pashmina Shawls

15th century CE
Kashmir

Fine cashmere wool textiles handwoven and often embroidered with intricate designs.

Kalamkari

Ancient art form
Andhra Pradesh

Hand-painted or block-printed cotton textile using natural dyes and depicting mythological scenes.

Blue Pottery

14th century CE
Rajasthan

Turquoise blue pottery made from quartz stone powder, not clay, with Persian influences.
